Since 1996

A cart, a corner,
and good timing.

Frank Farmer started with a hot dog cart at State & Pierce because Purdue at 1am had nowhere to go. It worked. It kept working.

The cart became the truck. Frank's kitchen team trained at Johnson & Wales, which is a strange thing to be able to say about a place you find at 2am — and it shows. The Fats are engineered, not thrown together. His son AJ works the window now, which makes this a second-generation operation on the same corner it started on.

"Slinging great munchies since 1996."

100%

Clean inspection, no violations. Tippecanoe County Health Department retail food establishment inspection — ready to roll.

  1. 1996

    The cart. Frank Farmer starts selling hot dogs at State & Pierce Street.

  2. Then

    The truck. The cart becomes the black truck at 30 Pierce St, right across from Harry's Chocolate Shop.

  3. 2019

    On campus. Purdue Dining opens a Famous Frank's station at the Cary Knight Spot. Meal swipes welcome.

  4. Now

    Second generation. AJ works the truck, and Famous Franks 2 takes the whole operation on the road.
